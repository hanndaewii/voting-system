# Judge Guide — scoring with CWPC (one page)

## Logging in

1. Open the login page address your admin gave you.
2. Type your email → **Email me a login link**.
3. Open the email and press **Continue to CWPC**. (If your mail app
   opened the link first, press the button anyway — nothing is used up
   until you click.)
4. No link? Use the **6-digit code** from the same email instead:
   back on the login page, tap "I have a 6-digit code".
5. Still stuck? Ask your admin to resend, and check spam.

## Scoring

- Pick your contest at the top if you judge more than one.
- Tap a contestant to open their criteria. Move each **slider** (or type
  the number): **1 to 10, halves allowed** (8.5 is fine).
- Every change **saves by itself** — watch for **Saved ✓**. If you see
  **Saving…** or **Not saved**, stay on the page; it retries on its own.
  Don't close the tab while it says unsaved.
- Top bar shows **how many contestants you've finished** (a contestant
  is done when every criterion has a number).
- Scores save as drafts. Other judges can't see yours, and you can't
  see theirs.

## Submitting

1. When every contestant shows **Done**, press **Submit final scores**.
2. Read the warning: **you cannot change scores after submitting.**
   Press Submit only when you mean it.
3. After submitting, your sheet becomes read-only and says
   **Scores submitted**. Done — thank you!

## If something goes wrong

- Session expired? You'll get a sign-in prompt that **keeps your page
  and your entered numbers**. Sign in again.
- Submitted too early? Tell your admin BEFORE results are locked —
  only they can reopen your sheet.
- Use a phone or tablet in portrait; the buttons are big on purpose.
  If the page looks frozen, pull-to-refresh once — your saved scores
  are safe.
